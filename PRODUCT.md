# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally. Recruiters and hiring managers for internships and early-career customer-facing AI roles (SE / FDE / CSE) who arrive from a resume link or LinkedIn and give the page ten seconds. Networking contacts — often senior people Avery cold-emailed — who click through and need to leave with a memorable impression that fuels the follow-up conversation.

## Product Purpose

Personal site for Avery Romain: Amherst College '27, political science and Black studies, defensive lineman (#93), and builder of live software (finance, data, recruiting apps). The site exists to convert a skim into credibility and a resume view. Success is a recruiter opening the resume and a cold contact remembering the site when replying.

## Positioning

The defensive lineman who ships production software — discipline from the field, proof in the products.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: view/download resume.
- Secondary CTA: email.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: a D1-caliber work ethic pointed at building software.
- Belief ladder: (1) this person is real and distinctive — athlete AND builder; (2) the projects are live, not toy demos; (3) the discipline transfers — football rigor shows up in the work; (4) worth a reply / an interview.
- Proof on hand: four live apps (finance, data, college-football recruiting), Amherst athletics roster listing, resume at /resume.

## Brand Personality

Disciplined, physical, understated-confident. Athletic energy carried by scale, weight, and motion — not by sports-kitsch decoration. Speaks in short declarative sentences. Big when it matters, quiet everywhere else.

## Anti-references

- Not a sports-team site: no turf textures, scoreboard kitsch, ESPN broadcast graphics.
- Not gamer/edgy: no neon glows, aggressive reds, Twitch-stream aesthetics.
- Not corporate SaaS: no gradient heroes, no generic startup landing patterns.

## Design Principles

- Earn the scale: one huge moment per screen; everything around it stays quiet.
- Stat-sheet honesty: real numbers (jersey 93, tackles, live apps) beat adjectives.
- Motion is athletic: fast, weighted, exponential ease-outs — never bouncy, never decorative.
- The site is the resume: craft in the details is the proof of the "builder" claim.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA contrast on all text. Full prefers-reduced-motion alternative for every animation. Keyboard-visible focus states throughout.
